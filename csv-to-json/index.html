Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about converting CSV to JSON.

Does this converter preserve numbers and boolean types?

Yes. Numeric values and boolean strings (true/false) are automatically cast to native JSON numbers and booleans.

What happens if a CSV field has commas or line breaks?

Properly quoted fields with commas or internal line breaks are fully parsed according to RFC 4180 rules.

Is my CSV data kept private?

Yes, 100%. Nothing is sent across the internet; your browser handles all computation.
