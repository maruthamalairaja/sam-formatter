Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about data conversions and security.

Is my data safe and confidential?

Yes, 100%. SAM Data Formatter is built with a client-side architecture. When you paste or upload files, your data is processed only in your browser's local memory. No file or text is ever sent across the network.

Can I convert between Excel files and JSON/CSV?

Yes! You can upload .xlsx files or paste copied rows from Excel directly into the tool. You can also export tabular datasets directly as genuine Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheets.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No. SAM Data Formatter is completely free to use without registration, sign-ups, or subscriptions.

How are nested JSON structures handled when converting to CSV?

Nested objects and arrays are intelligently inspected and flattened into formatted fields so your CSV output remains structured and readable.
